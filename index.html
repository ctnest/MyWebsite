About CT Nest

A more transparent way to find your next place.

Frequently asked questions

Helpful information about using CT Nest.

Is CT Nest currently available across Nepal?

The initial launch is focused on Chitwan. The platform is planned so other districts can be added in the future.

Can I post my property?

Yes. Register or login, then submit your listing with photos and contact details. It appears publicly after approval.

Does CT Nest guarantee every listing?

Always confirm ownership, documents and property details independently before making a payment or agreement. A verification label should only be used after checks are completed.

How do I contact a property owner?

Open a property listing and use its available contact option to reach the listed owner or representative.
